# Screenwriting method provenance

Cinema Craft v6 adapts the film/short-film workflow and method organization from the user-selected [screenwriting-skills](https://github.com/jtydhr88/screenwriting-skills), pinned to revision `51115f18d160aa8b2ac5813e4432b6f0b425358f`.

`references/story-development.md` is an authored, self-contained Lumina adaptation. Its routing covers sw-workflow, sw-premise-theme, sw-story-structure, sw-character-conflict, sw-scene-craft, sw-dialogue and sw-format-adaptation, with conditional checks from sw-truby-anatomy and sw-genre-anatomy. It retains staged development, short-film entry, concise project state, stage deliverables and diagnostic revision while honoring this user's native approval, runtime and video-production requirements. It does not install or vendor all 26 upstream modules, their quotations, translations or case library. Reference-book/script passages and example plots were not reproduced. Upstream's NOTICE distinguishes those third-party materials from the MIT-covered architecture, workflows and checklists; those distinctions remain applicable.

The new Mood-board, native scene/shot design and image-refinement adapter is maintained as part of Cinema Craft. Prior project and unrelated third-party notices remain in place. Upstream copyright and permission notice for the adapted methods follows and is also retained inside the importer reference.
